Decision rule (fixed 2026-11-21, before any panel answer was read). Per answer slot (single case, or each side of a pair):
- 3 of 3 reviewers pick the key's option (or an alsoAccept option): CONFIRMED.
- 2 of 3 pick it: KEPT.
- 2 or 3 of 3 pick one other option: CHANGE the key to that option (disputed).
- Otherwise (no majority, or a majority says "open"): OPEN (move to the dilemma's open field; not scored).
A pair item whose two sides get different results takes the weaker one; if a side goes OPEN, the pair entry is removed.
